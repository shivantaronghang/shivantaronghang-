Professional IMRaD Structure for Academic Writing

Title

Enter a concise, descriptive, and informative title (avoid abbreviations or formulas).

Keywords

List 4–6 relevant keywords to help discover your article in searches.

Abstract

Summarize your research (150–300 words): purpose, methods, findings, and conclusions.

Introduction

Introduce your topic, highlight significance, cite background work, and define objectives.

Materials and Methods

Outline the materials, setup, and procedures to allow reproducibility.

Results

Present data, figures, and tables clearly—avoid interpretation here.

Discussion

Interpret results, discuss implications, compare findings, and note limitations.

Conclusion

Conclude with the study’s main contributions—no new data introduced.

References

Add properly formatted reference entries (APA, MLA, IEEE, etc.).
