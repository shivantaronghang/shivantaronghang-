About the Meetup

The Innovation & Entrepreneurship Meetup 2026 brings together startup founders, investors, innovators, and aspiring entrepreneurs for a day of networking, learning, and collaboration. Organized by ONSNC Foundation, this event features pitch sessions, mentorship opportunities, panel discussions, and insights from successful entrepreneurs who have built impactful ventures.

What to Expect

Startup Pitch Sessions

Present your startup idea to investors and get valuable feedback from experts.

🤝

Networking Opportunities

Connect with founders, investors, mentors, and potential co-founders.

💼

Investor Meetups

One-on-one sessions with angel investors and venture capitalists.

📚

Expert Insights

Learn from successful entrepreneurs about building sustainable ventures.
