🌏 Global Youth Summit 2026

Empowering Young Leaders to Shape a Sustainable Future

About the Event

The Global Youth Summit 2026 is a groundbreaking virtual gathering of young leaders, innovators, and changemakers from across the world. Organized by ONSNC Foundation under the vision of Shivanta Ronghang, this summit focuses on creating ethical solutions for climate change, AI governance, and sustainable development as part of the Civilization 3.0 Mission.

What to Expect

🌍

Global Collaboration

Connect with young leaders from 50+ countries to co-create solutions for global challenges.

💡

Innovation Sessions

Participate in hands-on workshops on climate tech, ethical AI, and sustainable innovation.

🎤

Youth-Led Panels

Hear inspiring stories and insights from young changemakers and thought leaders.

🤝

Networking Opportunities

Build lasting connections through virtual collaboration rooms and networking sessions.
