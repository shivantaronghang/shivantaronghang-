Building Responsible AI for a Better Tomorrow

📅 November 22, 2026 📍 Guwahati, India + Virtual 🎯 Interactive Workshops

About the Workshop Series

The AI Ethics Workshop Series 2026 is a comprehensive program focused on building ethical AI systems, understanding algorithmic bias, and implementing responsible AI frameworks. Organized by ONSNC Foundation as part of the Civilization 3.0 Mission, this workshop series brings together AI researchers, developers, policymakers, and ethicists to shape the future of artificial intelligence.

Workshop Focus Areas

⚖️

Ethical AI Principles

Learn foundational principles for developing AI systems that benefit humanity.

🔍

Algorithmic Fairness

Identify and mitigate bias in AI algorithms and decision-making systems.

🛡️

Responsible AI Frameworks

Implement governance structures and accountability mechanisms for AI deployment.

🤝

Human-AI Coexistence

Design AI systems that augment human capabilities and promote wellbeing.
