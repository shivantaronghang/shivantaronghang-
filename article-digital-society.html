Introduction

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming governance by improving transparency and responsiveness. This article discusses AI’s role in enhancing democratic processes while ensuring accountability and human oversight.

AI as a Tool for Transparency

Transparency can be ensured through AI-powered data analysis and open government platforms. AI enables citizens to access real-time information on policies, budgets, and decision-making.

Ethical Framework

An ethical framework is vital to govern AI usage in public administration. This includes fairness, avoiding bias, privacy protection, and mechanisms for accountability.

Future Implications

The integration of AI in governance promises more efficient and participatory democracy. However, risks related to surveillance and algorithmic bias must be managed carefully.

Conclusion

AI has vast potential to revolutionize ethical governance, but the balance between automation and human control remains critical.
